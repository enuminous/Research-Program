# EMERG-GUTS-001 — Preregisterable protocol

## Phase A — Mathematical screening

- Freeze classical Einstein–Maxwell equations, conventions and initial/boundary conditions.
- Validate trace, units, conservation and geodesic-deviation identities.
- Require every candidate interaction to reduce to classical GR+EM when the new coupling is zero.

## Phase B — Numerical benchmark

- Start with the analytic radiation-era FLRW benchmark and finite-duration photon-pulse solutions.
- Document physical pulse energy, width, duration, geometry and observation scale.
- Measure geodesic deviation, not simply a chosen oscillator trajectory.
- Vary resolution and check convergence against analytic limiting cases.

## Phase C — Coherence ablation

- Define observable `I` from measurable field quantities using an explicit normalization scale.
- Fit the coherence model only on training data; evaluate held-out data.
- Compare B0, B1, B2 and (if mathematically derived) B3.
- Include a negative control with shuffled or time-reversed `I`.

## Phase D — FieldSpace

- Screen all 165 triplets for explicit E and M sectors: 9 eligible.
- Test E–M–S and E–M–X candidates individually; do not assume source-level closure.
- Penalize added parameters and report identifiability.

## Acceptance criteria

- No dimensional, conservation, or stability failures.
- Held-out prediction improves with calibrated uncertainty and survives negative controls.
- For a *physical-law* claim, an independently reproducible measurable deviation from established theory is mandatory.

## Status

Design only. No live Zoo, FieldSpace, laboratory, or astrophysical run is claimed in this repository.
